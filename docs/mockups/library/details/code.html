Barbell Back Squat

Quadriceps Glutes Hamstrings Lower Back Core Barbell Advanced

A fundamental compound movement targeting the posterior chain, specifically the quadriceps, hamstrings, and glutes. This exercise is essential for building lower body strength and overall power.

list_alt Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Unrack bar: Position the barbell across your traps and step back from the rack. Ensure your feet are shoulder-width apart.

2

Descend to parallel: Hinge at the hips and bend knees until thighs are parallel to the floor, keeping your back straight.

3

Drive up: Explosively push through your heels to return to the starting position while maintaining core tension.
